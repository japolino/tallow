I find Mara by the hives behind the cannery and just start helping her carry frames without being asked, chattering about how the fog here makes the clover taste like salt.
"You talk too much," Mara says, not looking up, but she hands me the smoker anyway. I take it as a win and ask her what is wrong with hive six.
I want to breed a new strain of cannabis. Something that only flowers in this fog. I am going to call it Saltwick Ghost.
I take cuttings from the commune's mother plants when nobody is looking and cross them with the feral hemp that grows in the cannery gutters, using my van as a nursery.
Three months later the first plants flower, pale as milk, and they smell like the sea. It worked. Mara is the first one I show.
I give Mara the very first jar of Saltwick Ghost. All of it. I tell her her bees did half the work anyway.
I convince the sheriff to leave the commune alone forever.
Mara kisses me. She says she has wanted to since the day with the smoker.
